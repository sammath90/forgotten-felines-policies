## Section 3 — Grounds for Euthanasia

*Tick all that apply. At least one must be ticked.*

**Medical / Quality of Life (Section 5.1 / 5.2)**

- [ ] Terminal illness with poor or no prognosis
- [ ] Severe injury — recovery not possible or would involve prolonged suffering
- [ ] Untreatable / advanced disease causing significant pain or distress
- [ ] Chronic condition — treatment ineffective and quality of life poor
- [ ] Persistent, unmanageable pain (confirmed by vet)
- [ ] Inability to eat, drink, or maintain hydration
- [ ] Loss of mobility or inability to perform basic functions
- [ ] Ongoing distress / fear / withdrawal despite intervention
- [ ] More bad days than good days (combined with veterinary input)

**Behavioural (Section 5.3 — all three must be ticked)**

- [ ] Serious and unmanageable risk to human safety
- [ ] Behavioural rehabilitation attempted or assessed by a qualified professional
- [ ] No safe alternative placement available

**Neonatal / Critical (Section 5.4)**

- [ ] Neonatal kitten unlikely to survive despite appropriate care
- [ ] Intervention would prolong suffering without realistic recovery

**Feral cat (Section 5.3 exception)**

- [ ] Adult feral cat — veterinary assessment confirms release not possible and welfare cannot be safeguarded within the 48-hour Standard 50 limit
